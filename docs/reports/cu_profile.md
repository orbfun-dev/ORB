Every instruction fits comfortably inside the 200,000 default budget; the
hot paths (`deposit`, `fulfill_settle` both variants) sit at 29–39% of the
strict 100,000 CU ceiling. Costs are **flat in entry count** by construction
(ADR-1: no instruction iterates entries), which is the empirical companion
to roadmap gate 5.10's 500-entry spam test — the CU of the 500th deposit
equals the first (28,875 vs 30,152 above differ only by the extension
branch's bookkeeping and the first-ever profile creation).

Phase 11 deltas worth naming: `close_entry` rose from ~7.5k to ~11.6k CU —
it now moves money (two pro-rata `entry_share` computations, the
`round_vault` debit, the `EntryRefundPaid` event) instead of reclaiming
rent alone — and `close_round` rose from ~10.2k to ~13.7k for the I22 dust
sweep and its Mega-Pot bookkeeping. `fulfill_settle` carries the four-way
split and the capped Mega split for +0.6k/+1.1k CU. All remain far inside
budget, and the keeper's ~11-entry close batching (Phase 11.8) sizes
against the new 11.6k-per-close figure.

Phase 11.6 (the universal profile, decision D1): `deposit` carries the
`PlayerEscrow` (init_if_needed) + `MegaPotVault` accounts on every call —
+9.8k CU of context deserialization — so a first-ever bet (profile
creation + the one-time fee transfer to the Mega-Pot) costs 30,147 CU and
every repeat bet 28,875 CU, both with 3.3×+ headroom under the strict
100k ceiling. The escrow path's fund/measurements moved for the same
reason (+mega_pot). A first-ever bet costs the player
`stake + 0.01 SOL fee (when set) + ~0.00174 SOL escrow rent + ~0.0012 SOL
entry rent`, and nothing beyond the stake + entry rent ever again.

### Auto-deposit batching (Phase 10)

`crank_auto_deposit` costs ≈22.3k CU per escrow (22,833 single; the 6-batch
totals 134,033 ≈ 6 × 22.3k), against the same account-metas overhead as
`deposit` plus the escrow debit/reimbursement bookkeeping. The measured
6-batch total is the basis for the keeper's
`CRANK_AUTO_DEPOSIT_MAX_PER_TX = 6`: it leaves ~66k CU (1.5×) of headroom
under the 200,000 default budget, so the batch needs no explicit
`setComputeUnitLimit`. Raising the batch size to 7 (~156k) still fits, but
6 keeps a comfortable margin for per-tx overhead drift; anything beyond 8
(~178k) crowds the ceiling and would require a configured CU limit.

## On-chain KAT replay (roadmap 5.13)

`kat_replay_on_chain` replays the committed cross-language fixture
(`tests/fixtures/entropy_kat.json`, 88 vectors) through the **deployed SBF
program**: one pre-crafted `Locked` round per vector whose
`total_lamports` is the fixture's sample total, the fixture's
`raw_seed_hex` in the mock Switchboard account at the round's own PDA, then
`request_randomness` → `fulfill_settle` on-chain.

Result: **72 of 88 vectors replayed bit-for-bit** (winning ticket, all three
cuts, Mega trigger flag, `randomness_seed_slot`) with 18 Mega-Pot triggers
fired; the Mega-Pot and treasury books are re-derived across the whole
replay and match on-chain state exactly (I2–I7). Runtime: ~2.3 s.

16 vectors are skipped solely because their sample totals
(`2^53+1`, `2^63−1`, the largest u64 prime, `u64::MAX`) exceed what the
test genesis supply (~10⁹ SOL) can vault-fund; their full-domain math is
already discharged by Phase 1's property tests over the entire `u64` range.
Phase 1's pure functions, the deployed program, and (in Phase 6) the client
now provably compute the same function on every replayed class: tiny, prime,
realistic, and boundary-ticket totals, trigger and non-trigger alike.
